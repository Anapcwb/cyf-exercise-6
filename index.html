Marilyn Monroe Exhibition at National Portrait Gallery

Marilyn Monroe: A Portrait

I recently visited Marilyn Monroe: A Portrait and found it to be a fascinating and insightful exhibition. The collection brought together an impressive range of photographs, and personal items that explored Marilyn Monroe's life and career. What struck me most was how the exhibition went beyond her public image to reveal a more personal side of the woman behind the icon. Seeing her clothing, scripts and candid photographs helped me gain a deeper understanding of her experiences and achievements. I particularly enjoyed the works by renowned artists and photographers, which demonstrated how Monroe inspired generations of creative talent. The exhibition also traced her journey from her early modelling days as Norma Jeane to her rise as one of Hollywood's most celebrated stars. I made this visit as part of my work to research Marilyn Monroe's life in greater depth and to explore the connections between her and Anna Freud. The exhibition provided valuable insight into Monroe's personal and professional journey, helping me better understand the influences, relationships and experiences that shaped her public image and enduring legacy.

Highlights of the Exhibition

  • Rare personal photographs and candid shots
  • Original film scripts annotated by Monroe herself
  • Iconic clothing and costume pieces
  • Artwork inspired by Monroe from renowned artists
